# Acorn Academy Sacred Art — Learning Sequence

These lesson pages teach biblical events, historical context, saints and traditional visual attributes.

1. [The Birth of Jesus](nativity.html) — learn the Nativity, Duccio's prophets and Botticelli's symbolism.
2. [The Shepherds](shepherds.html) — the Gospel narrative, artistic light symbolism and patron saints.
3. [The Magi](magi.html) — Matthew's text versus later three-kings traditions.
4. [Identify Saints](nativity-saints.html) — learn the distinctions between Anne, Catherine, Peter Martyr, Thomas, Anthony the Abbot, Margaret, Mary Magdalene and the two Johns.

The source paintings remain in the family's local Godot game. This public resource links to museum records rather than uploading reproductions whose public-site rights have not been confirmed.
